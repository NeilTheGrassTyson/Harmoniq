An H is two verticals and a crossbar; an equalizer is verticals of unequal height. Cut the right leg short, let a fourth bar stand free, and one shape reads as both. This is the only direction that continues something the product already ships — the three-bar glyph in EqualizerGlyph.tsx.

Bar-and-equalizer marks are the single most crowded shape language in music software — this is the safest of the three and the least likely to be mistaken for anyone else's only because the H is genuinely legible inside it.
