Octave — four forks
All four keep direction C's idea: two voices in a 2:1 ratio. What changes is how the square mark states it — as the wave itself, as an H built around the wave, or sampled into vertical levels. Accent moved to a neon cyan-blue; the small tile beside each mark is the true 16px favicon.

Where each one breaks
C1 loses its second wave below about 20px, and a lone wave is generic audio shorthand. C2 has the thinnest strokes of the four — the favicon here is already a bolder redraw, not the same file scaled. C3 reads instantly as music and not at all as harmoniq. C4 is the only one that survives 16px unmodified, but it is also the closest to the equalizer glyph the app already uses.
