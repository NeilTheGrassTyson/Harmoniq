Tradeoff
The idea is strongest in the wordmark. Alone in a square, the mark asks the viewer to read a letter — it works, but it earns recognition more slowly than a pure symbol would. The solid notehead is also heavier than the surrounding 500-weight letterforms; that contrast is deliberate, but it is the one thing to look at hardest.
