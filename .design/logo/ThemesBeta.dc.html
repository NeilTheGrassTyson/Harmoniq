Light is not a token swap

Every Signature hue was chosen against near-black. On white they fail outright — neon yellow measures somewhere near 1.3:1, which is not a contrast problem so much as an invisibility one. Light needs its own darkened hue set (shown here, and still needing a formal AA pass), and it has to drop the glow and the particles entirely: a halo on white reads as a smudge, and glitter needs darkness to glitter against.

Two meanings of "theme"

Worth separating before either is built. This is an app appearance setting — the viewer's choice, applied everywhere they look. BRAND_BIBLE §6 already promises something different: Harmony v2 profile themes, chosen by the profile's owner and seen by visitors. One is a preference, the other is self-expression, and they should not share a control or a token layer.
